# Teardown improvements — 29 October 2026

The first live removal exposed a queue dependency, shared identity-provider references, and a legacy bucket with no creation identity in its manifest. This patch moves their handling into the CLI. It does not redeploy the mailbox or perform additional live deletions.

- Owned Worker consumers are validated and detached before Worker deletion. Consumers are checked again before detachment, queues must be empty of consumers before deletion, and Access remains until the serving Worker is removed.
- Foreign consumers fail closed. A Worker deletion failure after detachment can be retried without detaching twice.
- Shared identity providers and Access policies are retained with an explicit reason in output and journals. Late references introduced during teardown are checked before deletion.
- `reconcile-bucket` previews original creation-audit evidence and supports prefix-confirmed application. `destroy --bucket-audit-id` performs the same guarded reconciliation when needed.
- Account/name/action/timestamp/audit-ID mismatches, absent evidence, and foreign or invalid markers are rejected without adoption. A matching marker left by an interrupted reconciliation is recovered without replacement.
- New commands support command-specific help, JSON/noninteractive use, and interactive input. Dry-run reconciliation never writes.

Regression tests use synthetic metadata and a fake cf executable; they do not modify Cloudflare resources. The actual mailbox remains undeployed.
